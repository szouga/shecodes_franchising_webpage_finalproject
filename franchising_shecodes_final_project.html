Tired of the 9-5? Own a Franchise!

Franchisee is a program that turns ambitious learners into franchisees or business partners by providing the resources necessary for them to be successful

What is a franchise?: A franchise is an already established brand.
What is a franchisor?: The owner of the franchise.
What is a franchisee?: An independent operator with the right to use the established brand's name, systems, and IP.
What are the eligibility requirements to join the program?: Proof of financial stability and a diploma or degree
What if I don't meet the eligibility requirements?: Email this address for individual review: sz@gmail.com

Pick Your Pathway

The paths to becoming a franchisee or an independent operator in the education space can range from operating private supplemental centers to complex
partnerships that deliver a university's degree programs.
In this pathway you will learn about: Individual investing, transnational education franchising, public-private partnerships,
subcontractual delivery and more.
Ever seen a fast food place in a grocery store? This is a symbiotic relationship between the host establishment and the subtenant. In this path, the host is a hotel, your brand is the subtenant, and your small business, (that you hope to turn into a franchise one day), is a Cereal Buffet Shop.
In this pathway you will learn about: Creating your brand/franchise, Host-Location Franchising, Non-Traditional Site Selection, how to pitch your brand, analysis of the risks and benefits for the host, your store and more.
Like watching movies? Although most major national chains are corporate-owned, becoming a franchisee for a luxury or niche cinema is still possible. Since these locations are more lavish and serve high-end food and drinks, its important to learn how to recreate that familiar experience and not just build another traditional movie theater.
In this pathway you will learn about: Basics and Combination of Real Estate and Hospitality, management of a theater, and more.
Think of your favorite mall or shopping center and ask yourself what is it missing that could make it better? In this path, learn the process of acquiring and managing a store, a food court vendor, and/or outparcel establishments.
In this pathway you will learn about: Diverse Portfolio: How to Acquire Multiple Establishments in the Mall, how and what role an establishment plays in the mall experience, and more.


Cider is an e-commerce clothing store. After recently opening a physical store in 2026, there likely could be another opening. In this path, learn how to negotiate why you are the perfect manager for the store and why the next opening should be in a mall.
In this pathway you will learn about: Project Management, The Art of Negotiation, and more.
